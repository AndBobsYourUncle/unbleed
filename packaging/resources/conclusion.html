You're set. Go stream.

  1. Open Unbleed from Applications and allow microphone and system audio access when asked. Look for the waveform icon in your menu bar.
  2. In OBS, add Audio Input Capture → Unbleed Mic for your voice and Audio Input Capture → Unbleed Desktop for game audio.
  3. Turn off audio on your screen capture source, and leave monitoring off.
  4. Optional: in Unbleed's menu, turn on Launch at Login.

Pick a different microphone any time from the menu bar. Everything Unbleed does is logged to ~/Library/Logs/Unbleed.log.
