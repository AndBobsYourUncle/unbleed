Unbleed

Your voice, without the game bleeding into it.

Unbleed listens to what your Mac plays and removes it from your microphone in real time, with a neural echo canceller, so your stream hears you and not your speakers.

This installs:

  • Unbleed, a menu bar app, in your Applications folder
  • Unbleed Mic: your cleaned voice, for OBS
  • Unbleed Desktop: everything your Mac plays (minus Unbleed), in sync with your voice

Audio on your Mac will pause for a moment at the end while the new devices load.
